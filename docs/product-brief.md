# MMO Crypto PvP Product Brief

## Vision

Build a browser-first MMORPG vertical slice inspired by Lineage II, Albion Online and arcade fighting games. The first market entry is Telegram + TON because onboarding, wallet connection and microtransactions are simpler than a Bitcoin-first flow.

## Player Loop

1. Open the game in browser or Telegram Mini App.
2. Create a character.
3. Move through one shared world map.
4. Farm monsters for gold and materials.
5. Fight players outside the safe town.
6. Lose or loot a portion of gold on death.
7. Convert earned gold into queued token rewards.

## First Milestone Scope

- One map with town, PvP area, dungeon area and boss area.
- 20-player realtime WebSocket room.
- Server-authoritative movement at 20 ticks per second.
- Arcade combat with attack, block, dash and one class skill.
- Monster farming and boss target.
- Inventory and gold.
- TON Connect UI and reward claim queue.
- Admin health dashboard.

## Not In First Milestone

- Real asset pipeline.
- Persistent accounts and character saves.
- Real TON transfer signing.
- Bitcoin Lightning settlement.
- Clan wars, siege, crafting markets and matchmaking.

Those are next milestones after the vertical slice is stable.
